## Goal
By the end of this milestone *Cavern Dash* exists outside Unity. You produce a standalone executable, launch
it by double-clicking, and play the whole game in it — menu, two levels, win screen, options, rebinding, a
best time that survives quitting. Then you make the repository something another person can clone, read and
build.

## Prerequisite
M12's gate passed: the options screen sets volume and rebinds controls, and everything persists.
